# Tutor Payout Setup (No Stripe Connect)

## Status

Tutor Stripe Connect has been removed from the Mentrixa tutor flow.

- Students still pay through Stripe Checkout.
- Tutors receive payouts through their selected method (PayPal or bank transfer).

## Tutor Setup Steps

1. Open tutor dashboard payout section.
2. Choose payout method:
   - PayPal (preferred)
   - Bank transfer
3. Submit payout details to finance:
   - PayPal email, or
   - Bank account details
4. Complete identity details needed for payout verification.

## Payout Timing

- Earnings are calculated from completed sessions.
- A 7-day hold is applied after session completion.
- After hold clears, funds move into available payout balance.

## Notes

- No tutor Stripe Connect onboarding is required.
- No Stripe Express dashboard is required for tutors.
- Student checkout and payment capture continue to use Stripe.
